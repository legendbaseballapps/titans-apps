Screen lock: —

Each pitch is three taps at most: pitch type only when it changes, then location, then what happened. Nothing moves on its own — the at-bat ends when you tap a result, the hitter changes when you tap Next Batter, and once an at-bat is closed the Step 2 buttons go dead so a stray tap can't rewrite a settled pitch. Any earlier at-bat is still reachable: Go back stays on screen through the next hitter, and a batter who has hit more than once gets numbered chips beside his name. While you're looking at one of those, the panel dims and nothing you tap logs a pitch. Trajectory is drawn the way you'd draw it on paper — grounders dashed, liners straight, flies arced, pop ups arced higher — and the result fills it in wherever it can, so only hits and errors need the four-button tap. Pitch type stays set between pitches, so a fastball-heavy inning doesn't need touching. First pitch swinger, chase, bad-on-breaking-balls, two-strike battler and sacrifice work themselves out from the pitches; late, speed, pull, bunt-for-hit, steal, aggressive and do-not-walk stay judgment calls. The export now carries every pitch location and spray point, so a game survives outside the app.
